# 0007: The board runs on a clock: quarter year steps, a log of actions, yearly frames and ledger

*28 November 2026. Accepted. Supersedes the "two-year turns" and "plain data, replayable" points of ADR 0006 for the board. Design: `docs/design/v2-board.md` section 16.2.*

**Context.** Phase 1 played the board in twelve two-year turns. Luke asked for time that flows: a clock that runs, pauses at any moment, and lets the player act while it runs (section 15). The game must stay deterministic and replayable, the Why? must stay complete, and the phase 1 rules must not change while the clock goes in.

**Decision.**

- **A fixed small step.** Time moves in steps of `game.years_per_step` (a quarter of a year) from step 0, May 2026, to step `game.steps` (96), May 2050. `tick` runs one step; `advance` runs until a hard stop, a chosen stop or a number of steps. There is no end of turn. Speed, pauses and the player's stop settings live only in the UI.
- **Only time.ts knows the step.** Rates are per year (`*_per_year`) and spans are in years (`*_years`), in the model and in the types. `stepsOf`, `perStep`, `yearOfStep`, `closesYear`, `yearsClosed` and friends in `src/board/engine/time.ts` are the only code that turns years into steps. No new rules went in with the clock: every per turn number became per year by halving, and every span in turns became years by doubling.
- **Inside a step** (`clock-step.ts`): visits lapse, builds finish, link quality, roots (R1 hook), held effects, events land, spread S1 to S3. Then, arriving at the next step: when a year closes, momentum (R2 hook), the promise, moods and Backing (scaled by the years closed), deals are judged; on an election step (the first step of an election year) the election is held; coins for the step; then the yearly frame, the year lines, the ledger fold, a chapter when a term closes, the forecast, the phase and the stops.
- **The log.** `BoardState.log` is every action as `{ at: step, action }`. `replay(model, gameNumber, start, log, stepReached)` gives a byte equal state. The run code is `b2.` and base64url of `[version, hash8, gameNumber, start, stepReached, dictionary, actions]`; a phase 1 code is refused with "This code is from an older version of the game."
- **Stops.** Every step reports its stops (`StepReport.stops`). `respond`, `election` and `ended` are hard (set in `game.yaml`, checked at build). While an event waits for an answer the phase is "respond" and `tick` and `advance` throw: every event must have a free response (checked at build).
- **Yearly frames.** `state.history` holds one frame each time a year closes, plus May 2026: 25 frames. "Four years ago", moods and Life in your years read the frame for a year (`frameAtYear`), never an index.
- **The ledger folds by year.** Within the current year every change is one record, so S1 and S2 can read the change in this step. When a year closes its records that share a council, quantity, rule, cause and link merge into one, at the first step it happened in. No sum changes, so every Why? still adds up. A Why? asks about a span of steps.
- **Proof it is a re-expression.** `clock-equivalence.test.ts` runs three phase 1 fixture games, captured with `turn.ts` before the change, at `years_per_step: 2`, and matches coins, every measure, Backing and young adults to 0.01 after every step. The games build nothing with a running cost or a coin effect, because those change by design from a lump every other turn to a steady charge each step. `clock.test.ts` checks that 96 ticks, one `advance`, a light `advance` and a replay all give the same bytes.
- **Phase 1 leftovers, kept behind their cards.** B1's carry over cap is judged every two years (`carry_years`), as the turn did, until package 8's purse replaces it. B3's weak hand applies while a model carries the B3 card, until P6 replaces it. Bots decide in the last step of each two year block and at hard stops, until package 9 moves them to "when a year closes".

**Consequences.** A game is about 96 steps instead of 12, so the engine clones the state once per `advance` and steps in place, and `light` skips building report lines. The phase 1 screens in `src/board/ui/` no longer typecheck against these types and are rewritten by package 11 (see `src/board/NEEDS.md`). Phase 2 rules (R1 to R4, E2, P6, ADR 0008) plug into typed hooks the clock already calls, each behind its rule card.
