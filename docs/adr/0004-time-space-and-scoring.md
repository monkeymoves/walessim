# 0004: Six four-year terms, 22 authorities, Lived and Left

*26 November 2026. Accepted.*

**Time.** The Senedd moved to four-year terms from the May 2026 election. Yfory short mode is six terms: 2026, 2030, 2034, 2038, 2042, 2046, ending in 2050, one generation. A term is the engine's step. Delays are counted in terms.

**Space.** The model's state is held per local authority (22) plus national stocks. Players choose "where" across four regions (North, Mid, South West, South East, matching the Corporate Joint Committee areas) or a single authority, depending on the card. Authored places (the Rhondda, Dyffryn Ogwen) are zoom views inside their authority; their decisions return a delivery grade (poorly, adequately, well) that scales the card's effect for that authority. Place figures are never summed into national numbers.

**Scoring.** Seven strands (0 to 10 pips) and Trust. Lived = how people fared during your terms (strand pips averaged over the six terms). Left = what 2050 inherits (strand pips in 2050 adjusted by legacy stocks: nature condition, carbon, coal tip liability, housing, Welsh speakers, debt). Your result is the lower of the two, per strand and overall. No single weighted score unless the player sets weights.

**Coal tips.** The Welsh Government disused coal tips register is licensed for personal use only, so it is not used. Tip locations come from open proxies (BGS National Landslide Database records naming tips, OpenStreetMap), labelled as such, until permission is obtained.
