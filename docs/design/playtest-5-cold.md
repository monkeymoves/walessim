## Must fix (contradictions and broken trust in the numbers)

1. **The scores disagree.** The top bar's Left for 2050 read 43, 41 and 38 while the end screen said "You hand on about 46" or 43, and "Both scores rose" when one went 46 to 46. The end screen's "Somewhere between about 0 and 5 down" is unexplained. (B 62, 71; C 59, 60; A 107, 110)
2. **Visit grade contradicts its tests.** "WELL" or "It went OK" above "Can young people stay? Not yet... about 0 times in 10"; "Every test passed" beside "House prices pull away: one homes pick is not enough"; "BADLY... The river needed: nothing" after picking the river. (A 44, 79; B 18, 55; C 39)
3. **Result slips contradict their arrows.** "Nature and floods stayed the same... pulled it down" with a falling arrow; "Fairness stayed the same. a big employer leaving pushed it up"; the cause starts lower case. (A 56; B 63, 69; C 47, 56)
4. **The verdict contradicts the timeline.** The reason blames a 2034 or 2042 crisis while the timeline marks "Worst turn" in 2030 or 2046. "Next time: play more cards that last" shown to a player who did. (A 110; C 58, 59)
5. **"You were ready"** still stamped when no coins were saved and all the damage hit. "Spent 1 coin: a quarter of the damage hit" when 1 coin halves it. (A 85, 96)
6. **Saving coins.** "Save 4 for a crisis" offered when only 2 carry over; the Welsh sheet offers "Cadw 3" without the explanation; "1 carried over" then the next turn shows 8 coins with no carry line. (A 56; B; C)
7. **The guide points at the wrong card.** "This one is slow. It works from 2034" pinned beside Build flood walls, which works now. Two guide notes stacked so one blocks the other's "Got it"; a note covers the Play button and the Life now box. (A 17, 30, 50; B 06; C 05, 06, 31b)
8. **Previews hide downsides.** Skills and apprenticeships previews only Work and pay up; the result then says it hurt Homes and community and Welsh and culture. (C 40)
9. **Promise helper lists** look wrong (Warm homes listed as helping Work and pay). (C)
10. **End of game wording.** "Next turn you can play 4 cards" after the last turn; "Hand Wales on to 2050" while the year already reads 2050. (B; C 56, 57)
