## Unclear but not contradictory

- The card cap (Trust) against 8 coins: spare coins feel wasted; show the cap beside the coins from the start.
- The two scores and the 2050 marker need one plain line in turn 1, not turn 3.
- Region versus all of Wales: say on the card what the choice changes.
- Previews say "If you play this in South East Wales" before a region is picked.
- "The catch" hides needs ("only at full strength if you have played Skills").
- Insider words still visible: Heddiw, Yfory, "coef", "Unexpected", "ranked 121 of 1,909 (WIMD 2019)", unlabelled Rhondda columns, hectares.
- Bars move in whole points, so most turns look like nothing happened.
