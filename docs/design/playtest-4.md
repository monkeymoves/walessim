# Playtest 4: Etifeddiaeth, confirmation pass

*Played 27 November 2026 against the build in the clean worktree `deploy4` at commit 1316228 (served with `vite preview`), driven through Playwright. Three sessions: a careful full game at 1440x900 (one Rhondda visit, two Bethesda visits, three shocks, every thread pulled, the letter, with term 4 played in Cymraeg); a careless full game (a standing ticket every term, debts, repairs never done, every shock absorbed, no visits); and a term in Cymraeg from the title screen at 1280x800 in a fresh browser session with the guide left on. Console errors, page errors, failed requests and 4xx responses were captured for the life of every page: there were none (the listener was checked with a deliberate error at the end). I did not read the code. This was a shorter pass: I did not revisit the atlas or the model book. Screenshots are in `design/shots-build/playtest-4/`.*

## Score: 8.5 out of 10

A confident 8, and a little over. Last time the 8 was "narrowly" because of three things in the first two minutes. All three are fixed, and nothing of that weight has replaced them. What is left is one layout bug that only appears when two places ask in the same term, and a list of small things.

What I saw this time. The preview row tells the truth: "Nothing this term. Lands 2030: Healthier up, under a pip; More equal up, about 1 pip" for Make coal tips safe; "Lands after 2050: counts in Left" for late slow cards; "If you play Land a big employer: Prosperous up, about 2 pips; Global down, about 1 pip" for a fast one. Every guide note was visible and clickable in English at 1440x900 and in Welsh at 1280x800. Invitations give their reason ("Because you played Homes for locals and Community-owned energy. They are delivered there this term"), and when two places qualified I was offered both and chose. My first Bethesda visit held one test and was ADEQUATELY; I went back the next term, added the river, a second homes thread and the levy trust to the three already in place, and it came back WELL with all three tests held. That is the first time Bethesda has felt solvable by thinking, and it was the best ten minutes of the four playtests. The promise result led the Trust reasons in every term. The filler slip appeared once in six terms instead of most of them. The careful game ended on a letter I had not seen before, "The gift": "My parents talk about your years as the time the town got its breath back. Work, warmth, and a bus that came... You made 5 promises out loud and kept 5... Maerdy Spoil Tips is grass now." The careless game lost the seat in 2042 and its letter noticed something I had not expected it to notice: I had held pips back every term and never spent them on a shock, and it closed with "I would like to know what you thought you were saving it for."

Across four playtests I have now drawn four different letters (The hollow years twice, Held steady, The uneven hand, The gift), each of which fitted the game that produced it. That is the replay hook working.

## What still stands between this and a comfortable 9

None of these threatens the 8. In order of effect:

1. **Two invitations do not fit (quick).** When both places ask, show the two "Go to" buttons and "Not this term" without scrolling: shorten each card to a heading, one reason line and its button.
2. **The last hand (quick).** Guarantee one card in term 6 that both pays in time and does something on its own, so the final term is a real choice between a visible win and a gift.
3. **A top grade (quick).** VERY WELL when every test holds with room to spare. At the moment a third Rhondda visit has nothing to aim for.
4. **Show Bethesda's term condition on the board (quick).** "This term: prices pull away, one Homes thread is not enough" belongs beside "In play this term" before the player chooses.
5. **Two preview leftovers (quick).** Empty preview for Regional compact; one slow card showing a small move against its ticket.
6. **The opposition benches (small balance point).** One caretaker term took Trust from Collapsed to Fair in both playtests. Thin would keep the weight of having lost the seat. The header still says "2 cards this term" while the caretaker is playing.
7. **Shock variety (small).** The careless game had Recession land in 2034 and again in 2038.
8. **Welsh (quick, needs a Welsh speaker).** The three items under 14.
9. **Copy (quick).** The loans line; "Put off the repairs lifted More equal" needs either a reason or removing.
