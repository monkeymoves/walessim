Caveats
- The NRW attribution statement says "All rights Reserved" while the licence field on the same page says OGL. This is the same wording already accepted for the flood zone layer in docs/data-licences.md; the licence field governs.
- Cadw's attribution asks for the date the data was received. If the pipeline is re-run, update the date in the attribution and in the `cadw_listed_buildings` source.
- The boundary layer page states no attribution wording, so the standard OGL line is used.
- The Forestry Commission attribution must appear wherever woodland cover is shown. The earlier fallback layer `geonode:nfi_wales_2020` was not used.
- WFD Cycle 3 interim 2024 river classification was not used: its layer page states no licence (see the plan's rejects).
